★ The details

Logistics & Information

What is the minimum order size?

Our minimum order size is 50 cans per event. This requirement allows us to fully customize your label artwork, curate the drink recipes, and prep the mobile canning equipment specifically for your activation.

How much does it cost?

Drink prices start at just $4.80 each. Pricing varies depending on the specific ingredients/recipe chosen, label customization complexity, and the total volume ordered. Fill out our contact form to get a personalized quote!

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes! Poptop is a fully licensed and insured business operating in Alberta. We carry comprehensive general liability insurance to ensure safety, professionalism, and absolute peace of mind for you and your venue.

Are your bartenders certified?

Absolutely. All of our bartenders are AGLC ProServe certified. Whether serving cocktails or managing mocktail activations, our staff is fully qualified to serve responsibly and handle alcohol service protocols.

Do you have food safety certifications?

Yes. Our team members are also certified food handlers. We handle all ingredients, juices, garnishes, and beverage prep under strict health and safety standards to guarantee top-tier cleanliness and product quality.

What areas do you serve?

While we are proud to be Edmonton-based and frequently serve the surrounding local communities, we are open to travel and opportunities everywhere. Let us know where your event is, and we'll work out the travel details.
